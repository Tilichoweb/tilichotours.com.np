Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Kande to Forest Camp (1770m → 2950m | 5–6 hrs trek)
Drive from Pokhara to Kande and start trekking through Australian Camp and rhododendron forest. Enjoy panoramic views of Annapurna range and Pokhara valley. Continue uphill through peaceful forest trails to reach Forest Camp for overnight stay.

Day 2 – Forest Camp to High Camp (2950m → 3700m | 5–6 hrs trek)
Trek through dense rhododendron forest and gradually reach Low Camp and Badal Danda. Enjoy close views of Machhapuchhre and Annapurna range above clouds. Continue ridge walk to High Camp for overnight stay.

Day 3 – High Camp to Mardi Base Camp & return (3700m → 4500m | 6–7 hrs trek)
Early morning hike to viewpoint for sunrise over Annapurna Himalayas. Continue to Mardi Base Camp (4500m) with 360° Himalayan views including Machhapuchhre, Annapurna South, and Hiunchuli. Return to Badal Danda for overnight stay.

Day 4 – Badal Danda to Siding & Pokhara (3 hrs trek + 3 hrs drive)
Steep descent through forest and villages to Siding village. After lunch, drive back to Pokhara by jeep with beautiful countryside views.
