✈️ What is an Ultralight Flight & Its Operational Timings?

An Ultralight Flight is a premium, single-person mountain flight tailored for one pilot and one passenger. Operating with less than 600kg total weight capacity and a maximum fuel capacity of 90 liters, these agile delta-wing aircraft maintain safe average cruising speeds of about 80km/hr up to an enforced legal limit of 130 km/hr.

Flights run Everyday across convenient operational windows: Morning shifts from 6 AM to 11 AM and clear Evening shifts between 3 PM to 6 PM. All flights conveniently launch from and land directly back at the Pokhara Domestic Airport tarmac corridors.

⚖️ Approximate Passenger Weight & Age Limits

Because ultralight aircraft depend on structural balance, weight and age compliance profiles are strictly observed. Each flight operates as a dedicated **single-person mountain flight** matching one client per captain.

Passengers must be **above 7 years of age** to be cleared for flight booking registration. Any minor child **under 12 years old requires explicit parental authorization** forms signed by parents or legal guardians before a boarding pass can be generated. Additionally, individual passengers must comply with weight metrics—requiring a minimum body weight of **25 KG** up to a maximum security clearance threshold of **95 KG to 100 KG**.

🛡️ Worldwide Aviation Safety Standards & Architecture

Because of low aerodynamic weight matrices, microflight aviation carries magnificent design benefits compared to commercial aircraft. High gliding values enable these delta-wing units to execute safe glide configurations and land securely even if engines are completely shut off.

✈️ Airfield Performance

Ultralights require minimal technical infrastructure, needing only a short 300-meter airfield runway clearance space to safely take off and touch down.

✓ Automated BRS Parachutes

The structural frame of each ultralight is fitted with a rocket-activated Ballistic Recovery System (BRS) parachute capable of bringing down the entire aircraft safely in an emergency.

✓ Global Multinational Crews

Flights are conducted strictly by veteran captains coming from all around the world with thousands of certified flight hours, including ex-Air Force aviators.

📋 Rules, Regulations & Cancellation Policies

✕ Flight Cancellation & Refund Terms

Voluntary Cancellations: Cancellations made up to 24 hours prior to the flight schedule incur a 25% administrative fee. Cancellations made inside 24 hours are entirely non-refundable.

Takeoff Rule: Once you clear airport security and arrive at the hangar runway area, the booking cannot be voluntarily cancelled for a refund. However, you are permitted to reschedule or transfer the passenger name at zero extra cost.

⚠️ Health, Fitness & Transportation Conditions

Medical Fitness: Passengers suffering from severe heart conditions, acute epilepsy, advanced respiratory issues, or pregnancy are strictly prohibited from flying for safety reasons.

Weather Delays: If the airport tower holds or delays takeoffs due to low visibility or tracking storms, your flight slot will instantly be shifted to the next clear weather window or given a full refund.
