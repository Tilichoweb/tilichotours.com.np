🛡️ Strict Eligibility Protocols & Sizing Enforcements

⚖️ Weight Metrics

Individual jumper weight must be a minimum of 35 KG up to a maximum safety threshold of 115 KG.

⏱️ Age Regulations

Available for adventurers aged 12 years and above. Minors under 18 require parental manifest clearance signature checks.

🎒 Footwear Rules

Closed, laced athletic shoes or sneakers are mandatory. Slippers, heels, or loose footwear are strictly banned from the platform.

📋 Ground Rules & Cancellation Frameworks

✕ Cancellation Conditions

Cancellations filed up to 24 hours prior to departure receive a full refund minus a 10% banking processing fee. Cancellations under 24 hours or platform stage retreats are completely non-refundable.

⚠️ Health Medical Warnings

Not suitable for individuals with chronic high blood pressure, heart disorders, neurological epilepsy, recent surgical dislocations, or pregnancy.
