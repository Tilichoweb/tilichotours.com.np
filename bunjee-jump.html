❓ Top 10 High-Search Safety & Logistics FAQs

Instant answers to the internet's most trending bungee jumping inquiries:

1. Is bungee jumping in Nepal safe?

Yes, absolutely. All sites utilize European Swiss/French equipment standards, dual redundant full-body anchoring harnesses, and rigorous morning load tests supervised by certified jump masters.

2. What is the official Pokhara bungee jumping price?

The base cost is NRS 6,000 at the Hemja Tower location. Premium river-touch configurations at Seti River cost NRS 7,500, which includes two-way Lakeside shuttle vehicle transits and digital media files.

3. What is the height of the Kushma bungee jump?

The Cliff bungee platform in Kushma stands at an incredible height of 228 meters (748 feet) above the Kaligandaki River, making it officially the world's second-highest commercial bungee jumping site.

4. What are the weight limits for bungee jumping in Nepal?

To meet strict structural cord calibrations, participants must weigh a minimum of 35 KG (77 lbs) and cannot exceed the maximum safety threshold of 115 KG (253 lbs).

5. How long does the entire bungee trip take from Pokhara?

Hemja and Seti River packages take roughly 2 to 3 hours total including round-trip transits. The high-altitude Kusma canyon excursion requires a full-day itinerary (approx. 7-8 hours total journey time).

6. Can we book a tandem double swing or couple jump?

Yes, both Go Bungee Pokhara (Seti River) and The Cliff (Kusma) feature purpose-built dual-rigging structures optimized to support tandem couple jumps up to a combined maximum weight limit of 140 KG.

7. What clothing and footwear should I wear on jump day?

Tight, lace-up athletic running sneakers are mandatory. Wear comfortable, form-fitting sports clothing. Skirts, loose dresses, and dangling jewelry are strictly prohibited on the platform.

8. Where exactly is the absolute highest water-touch bungee?

The Go Bungee site situated inside Pokhara's Seti River gorge operates a specialized 101-meter suspension line setup engineered explicitly to allow precision water dipping at the lowest elongation point.

9. What medical conditions restrict me from booking a jump?

People with high blood pressure, complex cardiac conditions, epilepsy, advanced pregnancy, or chronic back/spinal injuries are restricted from participating for health safety preservation.

10. Are photos and high-definition videos included in the price?

Yes. All booking configurations managed via Tilicho Tours natively integrate the complete digital media package, featuring wide-angle GoPro footage and high-shutter digital DSLR photography files.
