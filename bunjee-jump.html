🛡️ Safety Protocols & Secure Regulations

📢 Mandatory Health Exclusion Criteria:

To guarantee absolute safety, jumping is strictly forbidden for passengers facing pregnancy, advanced heart disease, severe high blood pressure, neurological epilepsy, recent orthopaedic fractures, or severe spinal complications.

Cord Auditing

Bungee lines are measured meticulously loop-by-loop and retired immediately upon reaching standardized structural cycle count thresholds limits.

Anemometer Monitoring

Digital wind meters continuously stream layout metrics. Operations freeze if crosswinds spike past standard launch thresholds.

🔄 Rescheduling Policy

Free modifications up to 48 hours before launch shift. Weather cancelations get priority slots or complete standard transaction rollbacks.

🎒 Belongings Protocol

All pockets must be cleared. Locker systems are provided free at the base counter to protect phones, glasses, and personal keys.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is high-definition photo and video media included?

Yes, professional media teams track your entire jump progression with high-speed DSLR setups and close-range action cameras. Digital packages are delivered straight to your smartphone coordinates immediately after completion.

Can I perform a tandem jump with my partner?

Tandem couple options are available exclusively at Go Bungee Pokhara (101m) and The Cliff Kusma (228m), provided the combined passenger metric profile remains within standard operating thresholds.

What clothing structures are recommended?

Wear fitted sports clothing or comfortable tracking attire. Avoid loose dresses or slip-on sandals. Secure, closed-toe athletic running shoes with laces are mandatory.

What happens if conditions turn bad?

If mountain squalls or thunder elements create safety risks, operations pause instantly. Passengers can reschedule to subsequent shifts or claim full financial credit refunds via Tilicho Tours.
