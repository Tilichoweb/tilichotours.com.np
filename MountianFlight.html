Frequently Asked Questions (Complete Directory)

1. How much does an Everest flight cost?

The standard corporate pricing strategy for the Everest Mountain Flight via Tilicho Tours is tiered cleanly by passenger nationality status: NPR 15,500 for Nepalese Citizens, INR 12,500 for Indian Nationals, and USD 290 for all international passport holders. These operational fees cover all applicable airport security taxes and ground transport inclusions.

2. Is the Everest mountain flight worth it?

Yes, it is highly recommended. It provides an efficient and physically accessible alternative to multi-week high-altitude trekking, allowing travelers of all ages and fitness levels to view the primary Himalayan chain up close in just one hour.

3. Which airline offers Everest flights?

Tilicho Tours operates in direct technical partnership with Nepal's premier domestic carriers, primarily Buddha Air, Yeti Airlines, and Shree Airlines. These lines use modern, twin-engine turboprop fleets optimized for high-altitude sightseeing maneuvers.

4. How long is the Everest scenic flight?

The gross time elapsed from runway takeoff at Tribhuvan International Airport to landing wheels-down spans approximately 50 to 60 minutes. This provides roughly 45 minutes of direct high-altitude cruising along the major peaks.

5. Can you see Mount Everest from Kathmandu?

No, direct ground-level viewing of Mt. Everest from Kathmandu is impossible due to the surrounding valley topography and intervening mountain ranges. You must take a flight to see it clearly.

6. What mountains can I see during the flight?

The standard flight path showcases iconic peaks such as Mt. Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,463m), Cho Oyu (8,201m), Nuptse (7,855m), Shisha Pangma (8,013m), and Ama Dablam (6,812m).

7. Is breakfast included after the flight?

Standard tickets cover flight logistics and hotel transport only. If you would like a premium package with an airport lounge breakfast or a landing lunch, please request a custom package from our booking team.

8. Is hotel pickup available?

Yes, our service package includes free ground transport in private, climate-controlled vehicles. We pick you up directly from your hotel in Kathmandu and return you safely afterward.

9. Is the flight safe?

Absolutely. The flights use advanced aircraft flown by veteran captains with thousands of hours of high-altitude experience. Flights only depart when clear morning weather and safe operating conditions are confirmed by air traffic control.

10. Which seat is best for Everest flight?

To maximize visibility, we utilize aircraft configurations with a single row of seats on each side of the aisle. Every ticket booked through Tilicho Tours comes with a guaranteed window seat.

11. What happens if the flight is cancelled?

If bad weather or low visibility forces a cancellation, you can choose to reschedule for the next available morning at no extra cost, or receive an immediate, full 100% refund.

12. When is the best season?

Flights run daily year-round, but the best visibility occurs during the autumn window (September through November) and spring window (March through May), which offer the clearest morning skies.

13. Can children join the flight?

Yes. The aircraft cabin is fully pressurized and climate-controlled, making it perfectly safe and comfortable for infants, young children, and elderly travelers alike.

14. Is passport required?

Yes, all international passengers must present an official valid passport at the domestic airport terminal security gate. Nepalese and Indian nationals may alternatively provide a valid government-issued photo ID card.

15. Can I take photos?

Yes, photography and filming are highly encouraged. The window layout provides an excellent vantage point for capturing clear photos of the mountains and glaciers below.

16. What is the best month for mountain flight?

October and November are widely considered the ideal months. The post-monsoon air is incredibly crisp and clear, providing the sharpest possible views of the Himalayan range.

17. How close does the airplane fly to Everest?

The plane maintains a safe, approved flight path roughly 20 miles from the peaks, flying at an altitude between 20,000 and 25,000 feet to provide spectacular, eye-level mountain views.

18. What is the difference between Everest Base Camp helicopter tour and Everest mountain flight?

The mountain flight uses a fixed-wing plane that flies a continuous loop from Kathmandu without landing. A helicopter tour is a longer experience that flies much lower into the valleys and includes actual landings at viewpoints like Kala Patthar or Everest Base Camp.

19. Is there an Everest flight certificate?

Yes, each passenger receives an official, personalized souvenir certificate signed by the flight crew to commemorate their journey to the roof of the world.

20. How early should I arrive?

Flights depart early in the morning. Our team will pick you up from your hotel between 05:00 AM and 05:30 AM to ensure you are checked in at the domestic terminal well before takeoff.

21. How much does the Mount Everest Mountain Flight cost?

Our official rates for the 2026 operating season are structured as follows:

  • Nepali Citizens: NPR 15,500 per passenger
  • Indian Citizens: INR 12,500 per passenger
  • Foreign Nationals: USD 290 per passenger

These rates include all airport passenger fees, ground transfers within Kathmandu, and our window seat guarantee. There are absolutely no hidden costs or surprise surcharges added during checkout.

Our comprehensive tour package includes everything you need for a smooth, stress-free experience:

  • Full 1-hour scenic mountain flight from Kathmandu.
  • A guaranteed individual window seat for unobstructed viewing.
  • Stunning aerial views of Mt. Everest, Lhotse, Makalu, and Cho Oyu.
  • Private hotel pickup and return transfers within Kathmandu.
