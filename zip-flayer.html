❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Zipline in Pokhara Nepal

Get instant clarity on your top travel search queries.

1. What is the official Zip Flyer Pokhara price for 2026?

The standard rates are 6,850 NPR for Nepali citizens, 8,850 INR for Indian nationals, and 8,850 NPR for foreigners. This price covers hotel pickup, safety gear, and the flight.

2. Is the zipline in Pokhara the world's steepest zipline?

Yes, launching from Sarangkot, it is known as the world's steepest zipline with a 56-degree incline and a vertical drop of 600 meters.

3. What is the exact Pokhara zipline distance?

The total distance spanned is an incredible 2 kilometers (approx 1,850 meters) from the top of Sarangkot down to the Yamdi Yamja side.

4. What are the daily pickup times for the zipline booking in Pokhara?

We provide three scheduled pickup shifts from your hotel: Morning at 8:00 AM, Midday at 11:00 AM, and Afternoon at 2:00 PM.

5. How long does the total zipline tour take?

The entire activity takes about 2 to 3 hours total. This includes the pickup, forms, 30-minute drive up, the flight, and the return drive.

6. Is there a strict zip line Pokhara weight limit?

Yes, for safety reasons dictated by Highground Adventures Nepal, passengers must weigh between 35 kg and 125 kg.

7. Is the photo and video package included in the Sarangkot zipline price?

No, professional action photos and videos are not included. They are an optional add-on available for an extra 1,500/- NPR.

8. Where is the landing site for the longest zipline in Nepal?

After your massive descent, you will land safely at the custom breaking deck located in the Yamdi (Yamja/Hemja) valley basin.

9. Is the best zipline in Pokhara safe for non-experienced travelers?

Absolutely. It uses advanced magnetic braking technology and heavy-duty harnesses. You will fill out insurance forms and be guided by professionals.

10. How do I make a zipline booking for Pokhara?

You can use the booking form above to select your date, nationality, and pickup time. Advanced booking is highly recommended during peak seasons.
