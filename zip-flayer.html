❓ Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Zipline in Pokhara Nepal

Get instant clarity on your top travel search queries about the zipline in Pokhara.

1. What is the current Zip Flyer Pokhara price for 2026 packages?

The standard 2026 booking rates with Tilicho Tours are NRS 6,850 for Nepali citizens, INR 8,850 for Indian nationals, and USD $8,850 for international global travelers. This flat rate completely includes your roundtrip transfers from Pokhara Lakeside hotels, safety gear use, and full adventure activity insurance forms processing.

2. What is the total zipline distance in Pokhara Nepal?

The best zipline in Pokhara spans an incredible distance of 2 KM (approximately 1.85 Kilometers). This makes it one of the longest, steepest, and most thrilling extreme gravity slides anywhere in the world, dropping over 600 vertical meters from Sarangkot down to the Yamdi landing zone.

3. What are the daily pickup times for the Pokhara zipline ride?

We provide three convenient scheduled daily pickup slots directly from your hotel front desk. You can choose from our Morning 8:00 AM shift, Midday 11:00 AM shift, or Afternoon 2:00 PM shift. The total experience takes around 2 to 3 hours from hotel pickup to final return drop-off.

4. Where is the exact launch pad and landing site for this zipline?

The thrill ride launches from the famous mountain peak of Sarangkot (known for sunrise views over Mt. Annapurna) at an altitude of 1,550 meters. It reaches speeds up to 120 KM/H before stopping safely on the custom breaking deck over at the Yamdi (Yamja/Hemja) side of the valley basin.

5. Is the action video and souvenir photo package included in the initial cost?

No, professional action photo and video files are completely extra and optional. You can view your dynamic shots on big monitors at the Yamdi landing center and purchase the entire digital bundle for an extra NPR 1,500/- optional charge if you love the footage.

6. Is there a strict zip line Pokhara weight limit for safety?

Yes, for maximum safety parameters overseen by Highground Adventures Nepal guidelines, riders must fall within a strict weight window of 35 kg to 125 kg (approx. 77 lbs to 275 lbs). Comfortable closed-toe sports shoes are highly recommended during your flight launch.

7. Is the Sarangkot zipline price safe for beginner travelers?

Absolutely. It relies on advanced European machinery technology, dual-core steel wire lines, and magnetic braking apparatuses. Before you drive up to Sarangkot top, you will stop at our central collection office to sign a registration ledger and complete standard travel safety insurance logs.

8. Can we combine zipline with other Pokhara adventure sports packages?

Yes! Tilicho Tours creates custom combinations. Many travelers choose to combine their Sarangkot zipline ride with early morning paragliding, ultralight flights from Pokhara airport, or specialized Himalayan helicopter tour packages to Annapurna Base Camp.

9. How early do I need to confirm my booking with Tilicho Tours?

Because seats are highly limited across our 8 AM, 11 AM, and 2 PM shifts, we strongly recommend making your reservation at least 24 to 48 hours in advance—especially during peak spring and autumn trekking seasons in Nepal.

Frequently Asked Questions About Zipline in Pokhara Nepal

1. What is the current Zip Flyer Pokhara price for 2026?

The standard price for the classic zip flyer in Pokhara is 6,850 NPR for Nepali citizens, 8,850 INR for Indian nationals, and 8,850 NPR (or equivalent USD) for foreigners. This includes your hotel pickup, safety equipment, and the massive 2km ride.

2. Is the zipline in Pokhara the world's steepest zipline?

Yes, the zip flyer launching from Sarangkot is renowned as the world's steepest zipline. It features an incredible 56-degree initial incline and a massive vertical drop of 600 meters, ensuring ultimate adrenaline for extreme sports lovers.

3. What is the total Pokhara zipline distance?

The total distance of the Sarangkot zipline in Pokhara is 2 kilometers (1,850 meters). It spans from the top of Sarangkot mountain all the way down to the Yamdi (Hemja) valley basin.

4. What are the available pickup times for the zipline booking in Pokhara?

We offer three convenient daily schedules for our adventure guests. You can book your hotel pickup time for Morning at 8:00 AM, Midday at 11:00 AM, or Afternoon at 2:00 PM.

5. How long does the entire zipline experience take?

The total time taken is between 2 to 3 hours. This includes your hotel pickup, a stop at the collection office to fill out insurance forms, a 30-minute drive to the Sarangkot top, the 2km zip flight, and the drive back to your hotel.

6. Is there a specific zip line Pokhara weight limit?

Yes, for strict safety reasons governed by Highground Adventures Nepal, participants must weigh between 35 kg (77 lbs) and 125 kg (275 lbs). You will be weighed at the collection office before the drive up.

7. Are photos and videos included in the Sarangkot zipline price?

No, professional high-definition action photos and videos are not included in the base ticket. They are an optional add-on available at the landing station for an extra 1,500/- NPR.

8. Where is the landing site for the longest zipline in Nepal?

After launching from the Sarangkot mountain peak, you will cross the beautiful valley at speeds up to 120 km/h and safely land at the custom-built deck in the Yamdi Hemja area.

9. Is the zipline in Pokhara safe for beginners?

Absolutely. The equipment utilizes advanced, world-class European safety technology and magnetic braking systems. You will fill out an insurance form prior to the flight, and professional guides will securely harness you in.

10. What is the best way to do a zipline booking in Pokhara?

The best way is to book directly through our integrated booking form above or contact our verified adventure travel agents. Due to high demand during peak tourist seasons, we highly recommend booking your 8 AM, 11 AM, or 2 PM slot well in advance.
