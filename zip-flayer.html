⚡ The Zip Flyer Experience – World Scale incline Velocity

Launching from the majestic ridge of Sarangkot at an elevation of 1,550 meters, Zip Flyer Nepal stands out as the most extreme zipline configuration on Earth. With an unparalleled 56-degree incline stretching over 1.8 kilometers of high-tension structural steel cable, participants plunge directly down a 600-meter vertical drop, reaching speeds exceeding 100 KM/H.

During your high-speed descent, you will catch panoramic views of the Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) mountain peaks. The extreme run finishes with a smooth transition into a custom hydraulic braking landing zone in the Hemja valley.

  • 📐 Track Vector: 1.8 Kilometers (1,850 Meters) Total Ride Distance
  • ⛰️ Launch Elevation: 1,550 Meters Above Sea Level at Sarangkot Station
