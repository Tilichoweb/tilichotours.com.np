⏱️ Step-by-Step Flight Execution Timeline

Our upgraded, readable logistics layout maps out your entire experience from your initial morning pickup to your final drop-off:

  • Step 1: Lakeside Hotel Pickup & Transit Registration
    Our air-conditioned shuttle arrives directly at your hotel door. You will be transferred to our collection office to fill out your required safety and insurance manifest forms.
  • Step 2: 30-Minute Scenic Drive to Sarangkot Hill Viewpoint
    Enjoy a smooth mountain ascent to the Sarangkot ridge launchpad (1,592m). Take in the panoramic views of the Pokhara Valley and the Annapurna range while your pilot monitors real-time wind directions.
  • Step 3: Pre-Flight Briefing & Harness Deployment
    Your pilot secures you into your flight harness and performs a triple-lock check. You'll receive a simple briefing on how to take 3-5 rapid running steps forward along the launch slope until the wing inflates naturally.
  • Step 4: 20-30 Minutes Premium Sky Time
    Glide smoothly through mountain thermals! Instruct your pilot on your preferred GoPro video capturing angles as you cruise directly alongside Himalayan eagles.
  • Step 5: Lakeside Beach Touchdown & Media Transfer
    Land smoothly on the designated dropzone beach at the bank of Phewa Lake / Pame side. Sit down, relax, and watch your pilot instantly copy all raw HD photos and videos directly onto your phone or send them to your email.
  • Step 6: 30-Minute Drive Back to Hotel Drop-Off
    Board our return shuttle for a comfortable 30-minute drive from the Pame landing zone back to your lakeside hotel room. Note: The total door-to-door duration takes 2 to 3 hours, depending on local traffic and mountain weather conditions.
