Classic Sunrise Trek in Annapurna Region, Nepal

Detailed Itinerary

We pick you up directly from your hotel lobby in Pokhara (820m / 2,690ft) early in the morning following breakfast. Enjoy a scenic 3 to 4-hour mountain drive winding alongside rapid rivers to the classic stone-paved settlement of Ghandruk Village (1,940m / 6,364ft).

Ghandruk is a massive, beautiful Gurung tribal village showcasing ancient traditions and museum houses. After enjoying a warm lunch here, your physical trekking steps begin! The route moves upward through deep, moss-covered rhododendron forests and past pristine mountain streams. It is a steady 3-hour trek inside the shady forest canopy until you arrive at the clearing of Tadapani (2,630m / 8,628ft). Stay overnight at a cozy local tea house lodge.

Wake up early for golden morning photo sessions as the sun hits the snow peaks right outside your window. After breakfast, we hit the trail descending gently into deep forest gorges before initiating a steep climb up toward Deurali Pass.

The trail passes deep wild creeks and high waterfalls surrounded by blooming flowers (in Spring season). This path provides regular windows looking out onto the high Himalayan horizon. After 4 to 5 hours of walking, you will enter the mountain ridge gateway of Ghorepani (2,870m / 9,416ft). Rest your legs, enjoy a hot shower, and gather your strength for tomorrow.

We wake up very early in the dark morning (around 4:30 AM) to start our steep hike up to the Poon Hill Viewpoint (3,210m / 10,531ft). The trail is a well-maintained stone stairway, taking about 40 to 60 minutes of steady uphill walking to reach the tower top.

As daylight breaks, witness a breathtaking 360-degree sunrise panorama over the giant snow walls of Dhaulagiri I and Annapurna I. It is one of the most famous viewpoints on Earth. After capturing incredible memories, descend back down to Ghorepani for a fresh hot breakfast. Pack your bags and begin your descent down the steep stone steps of Ulleri and Hile. At the bottom, our private sharing vehicle awaits to drive you safely back to your hotel in Pokhara.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Weather profiles vary significantly depending on seasonal frames. Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) provide dry trekking conditions with blue skies and maximum mountain peak visibility. Daily outdoor temperatures range from 10°C to 15°C, while night coordinates at Ghorepani can drop close to freezing 0°C.
High altitude tea houses provide highly diverse multi-cuisine menus. You can purchase standard traditional Nepali Dal Bhat (lentil soup with steamed rice and curry items), which provides excellent energy. Common Western items like fried noodles, garlic soups, oatmeal porridge, pan eggs, macaroni, and pizza variations are also available.
Mountain lodges offer clean, basic twin-sharing accommodations. Bedrooms generally feature two single beds with clean sheets, foam mattresses, pillows, and shared blankets. Bathrooms are standard shared configurations, and properties offer solar hot water hookups and Wi-Fi data access for a minor extra fee collected locally.
The trekking paths are classified as easy-to-moderate. The route utilizes well-marked dirt walking trails and stone steps, particularly along the steep staircase descent leading out of Ulleri village. No technical mountaineering background or ice axes are required—basic cardiovascular fitness is perfectly sufficient.
You can witness panoramic view coverage of multiple majestic mountain ranges, including two legendary 8,000+ meter peak formations: Dhaulagiri I (8,167m) and Annapurna I (8,091m). Clear conditions also highlight Machhapuchhre (Fishtail Peak), Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Nilgiri massifs.
The prime times correspond to March through May (when vibrant wild rhododendron forests blossom along the hillsides) and late September through November (which offers minimal humidity, low cloud layers, and clear, crisp morning horizons).
Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) is rare on this route. The highest overnight lodging point is 2,870m at Ghorepani village, and the brief sunrise peak excursion reaches 3,210m at Poon Hill. The gradual altitude progression allows your body to acclimatize safely and naturally.
