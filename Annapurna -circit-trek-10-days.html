Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Pokhara to Besisahar to Jagat (Drive | 2670m)
Drive from Pokhara to Besisahar and continue to Jagat by jeep sharing . Enjoy rivers, waterfalls, terraced fields and mountain villages on the scenic drive.

Day 2 – Jagat to Chame (15 km | 2650m | 5–6 hrs trek)
Trek through pine forests, suspension bridges, waterfalls and traditional villages with first beautiful Himalayan views.

Day 3 – Chame to Pisang (14 km | 3200m | 5 hrs trek)
Walk through narrow valleys and pine forests with stunning views of Annapurna II, Pisang Peak and Lamjung Himal.

Day 4 – Pisang to Manang (17 km | 3540m | 6 hrs trek)
Beautiful high-altitude trail with Tibetan villages, dry landscapes and panoramic Himalayan scenery leads to Manang village.

Day 5 – Acclimatization Day in Manang (3540m)
Rest day to explore Gangapurna Lake, monasteries and local Tibetan culture. Important for altitude adjustment.

Day 6 – Manang to Yak Kharka (10 km | 4420m | 5 hrs trek)
Gradual climb through alpine landscapes, yak grazing fields and peaceful mountain terrain to Yak Kharka.

Day 7 – Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi (7 km | 4505m | 4 hrs trek)
Short trekking day to reach Thorong Phedi, the base camp for crossing Thorong La Pass next morning.

Day 8 – Thorong La Pass to Muktinath (15 km | 5416m Pass)
Early morning climb to cross the famous Thorong La Pass with breathtaking Himalayan views, then descend to sacred Muktinath.

Day 9 – Explore Muktinath (3800m)
Visit holy Muktinath Temple, eternal flame and monastery area. Easy day for spiritual and cultural experience.

Day 10 – Muktinath to Pokhara (Drive | 7 hrs)
Drive sharing bus/van back to Pokhara via Jomsom and Kali Gandaki Gorge with beautiful dry valley landscapes and mountain views.
