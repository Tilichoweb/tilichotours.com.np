Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Pokhara to Ulleri (Drive + Trek | 9 km | 2050m | 5–6 hrs)
Drive from Pokhara to Birethanti and begin trekking through riverside villages. After Tikhedhunga, climb the famous stone staircase to Ulleri village for overnight stay.

Day 2 – Ulleri to Ghorepani (10 km | 2850m | 6–7 hrs)
Trek through rhododendron forest, waterfalls, and peaceful villages like Banthanti and Nangethanti. Reach Ghorepani and enjoy mountain sunset views.

Day 3 – Ghorepani to Poon Hill Sunrise – Tadapani

Distance: 12 km | Altitude: 2,670m | Duration: 6–7 hrs trek

Early morning around 4:30–5:00 AM, start a short hike of 45 minutes to 1 hour from Ghorepani to reach the famous Poon Hill viewpoint (3,210m). This is one of the most iconic sunrise viewpoints in Nepal, offering a breathtaking 360-degree Himalayan panorama.

At sunrise, the golden light slowly spreads over the massive Himalayan ranges, creating an unforgettable natural experience. After enjoying the view and photography, return back to Ghorepani for breakfast.

🏔 Poon Hill Mountain View Highlights

From Poon Hill, you can clearly see a wide range of Himalayan peaks including some of the world’s highest mountains:

  • Dhaulagiri I (8,167m) – 7th highest mountain in the world
  • Annapurna I (8,091m) – 10th highest mountain in the world
  • Machhapuchhre / Fishtail (6,993m) – Sacred iconic peak of Nepal
  • Annapurna South (7,219m)
  • Annapurna III
  • Nilgiri Himal (7,061m)
  • Hiunchuli (6,441m)
  • Tukuche Peak (6,920m)
  • Dhampus Peak (6,012m)
  • Gurja Himal & Tarke Kang
  • On clear days: Manaslu & Ganesh Himal can also be seen

The viewpoint is just a 45-minute to 1-hour hike from Ghorepani, making it one of the best short sunrise treks in Nepal.

After breakfast, continue the trek through beautiful rhododendron forests, stone trails, and peaceful Himalayan landscapes toward Tadapani village. The trail includes both uphill and downhill sections with amazing forest scenery and occasional mountain views.

Arrive at Tadapani by late afternoon and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere surrounded by nature and Himalayan peaks for overnight stay.

Day 4 – Tadapani – Ghandruk – Pokhara (8 km + drive | 822m | 3–4 hrs)
Descend to beautiful Ghandruk village, explore local Gurung culture, and enjoy mountain views. After lunch, drive back to Pokhara.
