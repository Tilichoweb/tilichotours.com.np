About the World Heritage Tour

Welcome to the Kathmandu Valley World Heritage Tour, curated by Tilicho Tours and Travel. Kathmandu is a living open-air museum where ancient history, mythology, and daily traditions blend seamlessly. This tour takes you across four monumental UNESCO World Heritage Sites, showcasing centuries of Newari architecture, sacred shrines, and unmatched artistic craftsmanship.

1. Kathmandu Durbar Square

History Malla Dynasty Royal Palace

History & Culture: Serving as the royal seat of the Malla kings, this historic square features grand courtyards, stone statues, and multi-tiered pagoda temples built between the 12th and 18th centuries.

Importance: It houses Hanuman Dhoka Palace and the Kumari Ghar—the residence of the Royal Kumari, the world's only living goddess, showcasing supreme Newari woodcarvings.

2. Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple)

Spiritual Hilltop Stupa All-Seeing Eyes

History & Culture: Perched atop a green hill west of the city, Swayambhunath is one of the world's oldest Buddhist chaityas, offering sweeping panoramic valley views.

Importance: Dating back over 2,000 years, the stupa's famous painted "All-Seeing Eyes" watch in all four directions, symbolizing wisdom, compassion, and the origin of the Kathmandu Valley lake.

3. Pashupatinath Temple

Sacred Shrine Lord Shiva Bagmati River

History & Culture: Situated along the sacred Bagmati River, Pashupatinath is the holiest Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva in Nepal, drawing spiritual seekers worldwide.

Importance: Its monumental golden roof and pagoda architecture stand as a profound center for Hindu philosophy, daily ritual devotion, and sacred open-air cremation traditions.

4. Boudhanath Stupa

Tibetan Buddhism Giant Mandala Prayer Wheels

History & Culture: Boudhanath is one of the largest spherical stupas in the world and serves as the global epicenter of Tibetan Buddhist culture outside Tibet.

Importance: Built along historic trade routes in the 5th century, pilgrims circle its base spinning copper prayer wheels and chanting mantras beneath fluttering prayer flags.
