Spiritual Importance

Muktinath, or Mukti Kshetra, is the sacred site where Lord Vishnu attained freedom from the curse of Brinda, symbolizing Moksha (salvation)[cite: 2]. For Hindu pilgrims, it is a life-changing experience to bathe in the 108 holy spouts and the two sacred ponds[cite: 2]. It is the only Divya Desam temple located outside of India, making it a supreme pilgrimage destination for devotees worldwide[cite: 2].
