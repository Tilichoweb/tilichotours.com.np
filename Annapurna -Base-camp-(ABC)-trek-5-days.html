Detailed Day-by-Day Himalayan Itinerary

Day 1: Pokhara to Samrong (Drive) & Trek to Chhomrong/Sinuwa (2-3 Hrs Trek | 6 Km | Stay: 2,170m)

Route Highlights:

  • Board your private 4WD rugged mountain jeep at Lakeside Pokhara for a panoramic off-road journey via Nayapul straight to the Samrong trailhead.
  • Cross the longest steel suspension footbridges hanging high over rushing rivers within the Annapurna sanctuary loop.
  • Hike up through lush green terraced fields and iconic ancient stone stairs to reach the beautiful Chhomrong village ridge line.

Day 2: Chhomrong/Sinuwa to Deurali Camp Station (12 Km | 6–7 Hrs Trek | Stay: 3,200m)

Route Highlights:

  • Begin your morning walking down steep village stone steps to the pristine Chhomrong Khola river.
  • Enter dense, quiet forest tracks and trace the roaring pathways of the glacial Modi Khola river basin.
  • Pass through the trail outposts of Bamboo and Dovan, resting near the historic Hinko Cave as you adapt smoothly to rising high-altitude metrics.

Day 3: Deurali to Annapurna Base Camp Sanctuary (10 Km | 5–6 Hrs Trek | Stay: 4,130m)

Route Highlights:

  • Trek past the Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC) landmark into the wide, alpine mountain amphitheater.
  • Witness the spectacular shift in terrain as lush sub-tropical greenery turns into dramatic glacier valleys.
  • Walk safely into the heart of the world-famous Annapurna Base Camp Sanctuary ring before twilight sets over the peaks.

Major Himalayan Peaks Visible directly from the ABC Sanctuary Ring:

  • Annapurna I (8,091m): The historic, legendary 10th highest mountain peak on Earth.
  • Annapurna South (7,219m): Imposing wall of ice and rock directly framing your western skyline.
  • Mt. Machhapuchhre (6,993m): The iconic, universally sacred "Fishtail" pinnacle peak.
  • Hiunchuli (6,441m) & Annapurna III (7,555m): Beautifully guarding the sanctuary gateway entrances.

Day 4: Annapurna Base Camp to Bamboo Forest Station (14 Km | 5–6 Hrs Trek | Stay: 2,310m)

Route Highlights:

  • Capture the absolute best panoramic golden sunrise photography over the massive Annapurna glacier basin.
  • Enjoy a warm, fresh breakfast surrounded by 360 degrees of pristine mountain faces.
  • Trace your paths back down the deep river valleys, exiting alpine borders into the thick air and comfortable climate of the Bamboo forest camp.

Day 5: Bamboo to Jhinu Danda Hot Springs & Return Drive to Pokhara (11 Km | 4–5 Hrs Trek + 3 Hrs Drive)

Route Highlights:

  • Hike over the rolling green ridges of Sinuwa down toward the riverside outpost of Jhinu Danda.
  • Unwind and celebrate your trek completion by soaking in the healing lakeside natural geothermal hot springs.
  • Board your return private or shared 4WD transit vehicle for a smooth highway journey back to your hotel at Lakeside, Pokhara.

Frequently Asked Questions — Annapurna Base Camp Trek 5 Days

Quick answers to the most-searched questions about express booking rates, permits, and trail conditions.

Can I do the Annapurna Base Camp trek in 5 days from Pokhara?
Yes, you can easily complete the Annapurna Base Camp trek in 5 days from Pokhara. This express route is optimized by utilizing local 4WD jeeps to bypass lower trail sections, letting you begin hiking directly from high trailheads like Samrong. It requires good physical health as daily hiking averages 5 to 7 hours to reach the sanctuary basin safely.
What is the total 5 days ABC trek cost from Pokhara?
The all-inclusive 5 days ABC trek cost from Pokhara ranges between **$260 to $380 USD per person** depending on your selection tier. Booking with Tilicho Tours covers local mountain guides, porters, transport, permits, and mountain teahouse arrangements.
What is the Nepal trekking permits price for the ACAP and TIMS cards?
The official **Nepal trekking permits price** for the Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) permit is **3,000 NPR (approx. $23 USD)** for international travelers, plus **2,000 NPR** for the TIMS registration card. Our agency handles all permit paperwork smoothly before departure.
How difficult is the short Annapurna trekking package to ABC?
The **short Annapurna trekking package** to ABC is physically demanding due to its faster altitude gain and thousands of steep stone steps (especially around Chhomrong). However, it is highly manageable for healthy walkers since it doesn't reach extreme pass altitudes.
Which is the best travel agency in Pokhara for the ABC trek booking?
Tilicho Tours, based in Pokhara near Lakeside, is widely trusted as one of the best travel agencies in Pokhara for the ABC trek. We offer certified local guides, premium safety tracking, and fully customizable private trekking footprints.
Is a certified mountain guide hire mandatory for the ABC trek?
Yes, current Nepal government regulations require all international trekkers in the Annapurna conservation sectors to travel with a licensed **mountain guide and porter hire in Pokhara**. This guarantees safety along higher glacial sections and supports local mountain communities.
What mountains are visible from the Annapurna Base Camp sanctuary ring?
Trekkers enjoy sweeping 360-degree panoramic views of massive peaks: the legendary **Mt. Annapurna I (8,091m)**, **Annapurna South (7,219m)**, and the sacred fishtail silhouette of **Mt. Machhapuchhre (6,993m)**.
What is the maximum altitude reached on this 5-day ABC itinerary?
The highest point reached on this tour is **4,130 meters (13,549 feet)** right at the main base camp marker. To ensure your safety at this elevation, our professional guides monitor your acclimatization levels closely along the trail.
What is the difference between the Full Package and Semi Option tiers?
Both options offer identical premium arrangements, including licensed guides, porters, transport, and teahouse stays. The only difference is that our **Semi Plan excludes trail lunch/dinner meals**, giving you the flexibility to order and pay for food directly at the guesthouses.
When is the best season to book an express short trek to ABC?
The peak windows are **Autumn (September to November)** for crisp blue skies and crystal-clear mountain views, and **Spring (March to May)**, when the valleys come alive with blooming wildflowers along the lower forest paths.
