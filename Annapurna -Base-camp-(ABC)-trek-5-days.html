Detailed Itinerary (Day by Day)

Day 1: Pokhara to Samrong (Drive) & Trek to Chhomrong/Sinuwa

  • Elevation: Pokhara (820m) → Samrong (1,780m) → Chhomrong (2,170m)
  • Distance: ~60km drive, then 2 hours trek.
  • Highlights: Drive to Samrong (near Jhinu Danda). Trek through terraced fields and forests. Option to enjoy a relaxing hot spring bath at Jhinu Danda. If time permits, continue another 2 hours to Sinuwa (conditions apply).

Day 2: Chhomrong/Sinuwa to Deurali (3,200m)

  • Walking Time: 6 - 7 hours.
  • Highlights: Start at 7:00 AM. Uphill trek beside the Modi Khola river. Pass through Bamboo and Dovan. Trek through lush forests to reach Himalaya for lunch. Pass Hinko Cave (3,100m) to reach Deurali, the gateway to the Sanctuary.
  • Stay: Overnight at a local guest house.

Day 3: Deurali to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m)

  • Distance: ~15km | Trek Time: Approx 5 hours.
  • Highlights: Trek through steeper terrain as green scenery transitions into rock and ice. Entry into the "Himalayan Heaven" with a serene, cold atmosphere. Arrive at the destination: the magnificent Annapurna Base Camp.
  • Stay: Overnight at a local guest house.

Mountains Visible from ABC:

  • Annapurna I (8,091m): 10th highest mountain in the world.
  • Annapurna South (7,219m): A prominent sight with a rugged face.
  • Machhapuchhre (6,993m): The iconic "Fishtail" sacred mountain.
  • Hiunchuli (6,441m): Neighboring Annapurna South.
  • Annapurna III (7,555m) & Gangapurna (7,455m): Stunning glacial peaks.
  • Tent Peak (5,695m): Also known as Tharpu Chuli.

Day 4: Explore ABC & Trek to Bamboo

  • Distance: 14km | Trek Time: Approx 5 hours.
  • Description: Early morning sunrise view over the Annapurna range. After breakfast, trek back downhill via MBC and Deurali to reach Bamboo.
  • Stay: Overnight at Bamboo local guest house.

Day 5: Bamboo to Jhinu Danda & Drive to Pokhara

  • Distance: ~14km | Trek Time: Approx 5 hours.
  • Description: Trek uphill to Sinuwa/Chhomrong, then downhill to Jhinu Danda. Enjoy the natural hot springs by the Modi Khola river. Final 30-minute walk to Samrong for a 3-hour jeep/bus drive back to Pokhara.

Important Notes:

• Walking hours include rest time. Breakfast ~7 AM, Lunch ~1 PM, Dinner ~7 PM.
• Transport may be on a sharing basis. Itinerary subject to change based on weather and fitness.

Frequently Asked Questions — ABC Trek 5 Days

Everything you need to know about the short Annapurna Base Camp package from Pokhara.

Can I do the Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) trek in 5 days from Pokhara?
Yes, you can absolutely complete the Annapurna Base Camp trek in 5 days from Pokhara. This short itinerary is optimized for time-bound travelers by utilizing local 4WD jeeps to drive past traditional trail sections directly to high trailheads like Matque or Jhinu Danda. It requires solid physical fitness as you will be trekking 5 to 7 hours daily directly to the base camp sanctuary and back.
What is the route for the short 5 days ABC trek itinerary?
The standard express route starts with a scenic drive from Pokhara to Matque via Nayapul. From Matque, you hike through Chhomrong and Sinuwa on Day 1. Day 2 takes you up through Bamboo and Himalaya to Deurali. Day 3 brings you straight to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) via Machhapuchhre Base Camp. Days 4 and 5 focus on a rapid downhill descent back to Jhinu Danda hot springs and a drive back to Pokhara.
How difficult is the 5-day Annapurna Base Camp short trek?
The short 5 days ABC trek is classified as moderately difficult to strenuous. Because the itinerary is condensed from the classic 10-day loop, you will encounter rapid altitude gain and sleep at high elevations quickly. The trail features thousands of stone stairs (especially around the Chhomrong village ridge). Regular cardio preparation and leg strengthening exercises before arriving in Nepal are highly recommended.
What is the total cost of a 5-day ABC trek package from Pokhara?
The total cost for an all-inclusive 5-day ABC trek package ranges between $350 to $450 USD per person depending on group size. This professional agency package typically includes your ACAP and TIMS permits, private/sharing jeep transport from Pokhara, experienced mountain guide services, porter allocations, and standard teahouse accommodation with standard meals during the trek.
When is the best time to go on a short ABC trek?
The peak seasons for the clearest mountain views and safest weather are **Autumn (September to November)** and **Spring (March to May)**. Trekking in spring offers beautiful blooming rhododendron forests along the Sinuwa trails, while autumn offers the crispest, cloudless blue skies for photographing Mt. Annapurna I and Machhapuchhre.
Do I need to hire a guide and porter for a 5-day short Annapurna trek?
Yes, under current Nepal government regulations, solo trekking without an authorized professional is restricted in the region. Hiring an accredited licensed guide from a certified Pokhara travel agency guarantees your trail safety, navigation through avalanche-prone spots above Deurali, and seamless management of limited high-altitude teahouse room bookings.
What permits are required for the Annapurna Base Camp trek?
You require two primary legal documents: the ACAP (Annapurna Conservation Area Project) Permit and the TIMS (Trekking Information Management System) Card. If you book your 5-day tour with us at Tilicho Tours, our lakeside agency office manages all permit paperwork and government verifications before your departure so you can trek hassle-free.
Is altitude sickness a risk on a fast 5-day ABC trek?
Yes, because you reach an elevation of 4,130 meters (13,549 feet) at Annapurna Base Camp within three days. To minimize Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) risks, our professional guides monitor your oxygen levels, enforce a slow walking pace, and ensure you stay properly hydrated. Carrying Diamox tablets as a safety measure is highly recommended.
What kind of accommodation can I expect on the trail?
You will sleep in community-run mountain teahouses (trekking lodges). Rooms are clean, basic, and usually feature twin beds with shared bathroom facilities. As you push higher into Deurali, MBC, and ABC, resources become limited, meaning rooms are frequently booked on a sharing basis during peak trekking months. Hot showers and Wi-Fi are available for a small extra local fee.
What should I pack for a short 5-day ABC trek?
Since it is a fast 5-day trek, you should pack light. Essential items include: a lightweight down jacket, windproof layers, thermal base underwear, broken-in trekking boots, a warm sleeping bag (comfort rated to -10°C), a water purification system, sunglasses, sunblock, a headlamp, and basic personal first-aid medicine.
