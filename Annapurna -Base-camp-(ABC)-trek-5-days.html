Detailed Day-by-Day Himalayan Itinerary

Day 1: Pokhara to Samrong (Drive) & Trek to Chhomrong/Sinuwa (2-3 Hrs Trek | 6 Km / 3.7 Miles | Stay: 2,170m)

  • Route Highlights: Board your private 4WD rugged mountain jeep at Lakeside Pokhara for a panoramic off-road journey via Nayapul straight to the Samrong trailhead. Cross the longest suspension footbridges in the Annapurna conservation region before hiking up through lush terraced fields to the iconic Chhomrong village ridge line.

Day 2: Chhomrong/Sinuwa to Deurali Camp Station (6-7 Hrs Trek | 12 Km / 7.4 Miles | Stay: 3,200m)

  • Route Highlights: Begin your morning descent down stone steps to the Chhomrong river before entering deep forest tracks. Trace the roaring paths of the glacial Modi Khola river basin, passing Bamboo and Dovan. Rest near the historic Hinko Cave as you adapt smoothly to rising high-altitude trail metrics.

Day 3: Deurali to Annapurna Base Camp Sanctuary (5-6 Hrs Trek | 10 Km / 6.2 Miles | Stay: 4,130m)

  • Route Highlights: Trek past Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC) into the wide, alpine mountain amphitheater. Stand in awe as the landscape shifts into spectacular glacier valleys. Walk safely into the heart of the Annapurna Base Camp Sanctuary ring before twilight sets over the peaks.

Major Himalayan Peaks Visible directly from the ABC Sanctuary Ring:

  • Annapurna I (8,091m): The historic 10th highest mountain peak on Earth.
  • Annapurna South (7,219m): Imposing wall directly framing the western skyline.
  • Machhapuchhre (6,993m): The legendary, universally sacred "Fishtail" pinnacle peak.
  • Hiunchuli (6,441m): Beautifully guarding the sanctuary gateway entrances.

Day 4: Annapurna Base Camp to Bamboo Forest Station (5-6 Hrs Trek | 14 Km / 8.6 Miles | Stay: 2,310m)

  • Route Highlights: Capture the absolute best panoramic golden sunrise photography over the Annapurna Massif. After breakfast, trace your path back down through the deep river valleys, exiting high alpine borders into the rich air and comfortable climate of the Bamboo forest camp.

Day 5: Bamboo to Jhinu Danda & Return Drive to Pokhara (4-5 Hrs Trek + 3 Hrs Drive | 11 Km / 6.8 Miles | Return: 820m)

  • Route Highlights: Hike over Sinuwa loops down to Jhinu Danda. Unwind in the healing riverside natural geothermal hot springs. Afterward, your private 4WD return transport takes you straight back to your hotel in Lakeside, Pokhara.

Yes, you can easily complete the Annapurna Base Camp trek in 5 days from Pokhara. This express route is optimized by utilizing local 4WD jeeps to bypass lower trail sections, letting you begin hiking directly from high trailheads like Samrong. It requires good physical health as daily hiking averages 5 to 7 hours to reach the sanctuary basin safely.

The official **Nepal trekking permits price** for the Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) permit is approximately **3,000 NPR (approx. $23 USD)** for international travelers, plus **2,000 NPR** for the TIMS validation card. Is a mountain guide and porter hire in Pokhara mandatory for this short trek?
Yes, under current Nepal government regulations, solo trekking without an authorized professional is restricted in the region. Securing a certified **mountain guide and porter hire in Pokhara** ensures your safety along avalanche-prone sections above Deurali and guarantees room availability during peak months.
When is the best time to book an express short trek to ABC from Pokhara?
The peak windows for clear weather and mountain views are **Autumn (September to November)** and **Spring (March to May)**. Spring offers blooming rhododendron forests along the Sinuwa trails, while autumn delivers crisp, cloudless blue skies for photographing Mt. Annapurna I and Machhapuchhre.
How difficult is the short Annapurna trekking package compared to the Circuit loop?
The **short Annapurna trekking package** is physically demanding due to its faster altitude gain and thousands of steep stone steps (especially around Chhomrong). However, it is much shorter and doesn't reach the extreme altitudes of the Annapurna Circuit (which crosses Thorong La Pass at 5,416m).
What is the maximum altitude reached on the express 5-day ABC itinerary?
The highest point reached on this tour is **4,130 meters (13,549 feet)** right at the Annapurna Base Camp marker. To ensure your safety at this elevation, our guides pace the trek carefully and monitor your acclimatization every step of the way.
