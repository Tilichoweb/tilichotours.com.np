Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Pokhara to Chame (Drive 175 km | 2670m | 8–9 hrs)
Drive early morning from Pokhara by sharing vehicle with Scenic drive via Besisahar, the drive by sharing jeep through rivers, waterfalls, and terraced hills. Reach Chame before evening, the gateway to the Annapurna region and enjoy the peaceful mountain village overnight.

Day 2 – Chame to Pisang (15 km | 3200m | 5 hrs trek)
Walk through pine forests, suspension bridges, and beautiful mountain valleys. Enjoy views of Annapurna peaks and explore the peaceful Buddhist village of Pisang.

Day 3 – Pisang to Manang (17 km | 3540m | 6 hrs trek)
Trek through Tibetan-style villages with amazing Himalayan views. Visit monasteries and enjoy panoramic landscapes before reaching Manang village.

Day 4 – Acclimatization Day in Manang (Optional 3–5 km hike)
Rest day for altitude adjustment. Explore Braga Monastery, Gangapurna Lake, and nearby viewpoints while preparing for higher elevation trekking.

Day 5 – Manang to Shree Kharka (10 km | 4050m | 5 hrs trek)
Steady uphill trail through alpine landscapes and peaceful mountain routes. Enjoy excellent views of Annapurna II and Tilicho Peak.

Day 6 – Shree Kharka to Tilicho Base Camp (8 km | 4150m | 4 hrs trek)
A rugged and scenic trail leads to Tilicho Base Camp. Rocky landscapes and dramatic mountain scenery make this a special trekking day.

Day 7 – Tilicho Lake Visit & Return (16 km | 4130m | 6 hrs trek)
Early morning hike to Tilicho Lake, one of the world’s highest glacial lakes. Enjoy turquoise water, snow peaks, and unforgettable Himalayan scenery before returning to Shree Kharka.

Day 8 – Shree Kharka to Yak Kharka (12 km | 4050m | 6 hrs trek)
Cross alpine meadows and yak pastures with quiet mountain trails. A peaceful day with beautiful Himalayan landscapes and local shepherd culture.

Day 9 – Yak Kharka to Thorong High Camp (9 km | 4925m | 5 hrs trek)
Gradual climb to High Camp for Thorong La Pass crossing. Spectacular views and strong high-mountain atmosphere before the biggest trekking day.

Day 10 – Thorong La Pass to Muktinath (15 km | 5416m pass | 7 hrs trek)
Cross the famous Thorong La Pass, one of the highest trekking passes in the world. Descend to sacred Muktinath Temple for overnight stay.

Day 11 – Muktinath to Pokhara (Drive 165 km | 7–8 hrs)
Drive through the Kali Gandaki Valley with changing landscapes from dry Mustang to green Pokhara. Relax at Lakeside after completing your adventure.
